# Fix 6.1/2: skipped count is by lines

Mutant 6.1/2 (`tests/corpus-eval.py` `cmd_aggregate`): `skipped += 1` replaced
by `skipped += len(vs.split())`, so the skipped count tallies verdicts, not
verdict lines.

## Case added

`tests/corpus-eval.bats`: `aggregate counts skipped verdict lines by line, not
by verdict`.

Verdicts file: one matched line (`000001.txt`, one verdict), then two unmatched
lines: `999998.txt` with three verdicts (`ro rw ff`) and `999999.txt` with none.
Two lines, three verdicts. The asserted line is exactly
`verdict lines skipped, no manifest entry: 2`.

State of the world that fails it: the skipped count tallies verdicts rather
than lines. What else could produce the 2: a count that ignores zero-verdict
lines would give 1 here, and a count that stops at the first unmatched line
would give 1, so the 2 is the line count only. The matched line does not feed
the skipped count, so its verdict cannot supply the 2.

## Evidence

- Unchanged code: `scripts/run-bats.sh tests/corpus-eval.bats` gives
  5 passed, 0 failed.
- Under the mutant, the case fails on its own assertion (bats log: `not ok 3
  aggregate counts skipped verdict lines by line, not by verdict`, failing
  `grep -Fxq "verdict lines skipped, no manifest entry: 2"`). Mutant output
  reports 3, the verdict count.
